Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting Amrah Resort & Camp.

Is swimming pool access complimentary for all guests? ▼

Yes! Swimming pool access is 100% complimentary for all residing guests staying in both our Swiss Cottages and Glamping Tents. Proper swimwear is required.

Do you arrange Ganga river rafting and bungee jumping? ▼

Yes, we provide end-to-end booking and vehicle transfers for 16 KM Shivpuri and 26 KM Marine Drive rafting, as well as Jumpin Heights Bungee Jumping (just 2.5 km from our resort).

What are the meal timings and menu items? ▼

We serve buffet meals: Breakfast (8:30–10:30 AM), Lunch (1:00–3:00 PM), Evening Snacks with Tea (6:00–7:00 PM), and Dinner with Bonfire (8:30–10:30 PM). Both Pure Veg and Non-Veg dishes are prepared in separate sections.

Is the road suitable for small hatchback cars? ▼

Yes, the road from Tapovan through Neelkanth bridge to Mohan Chatti is fully paved (pucca tar road) and accessible for all hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, and tempo travelers with dedicated safe on-site parking.
