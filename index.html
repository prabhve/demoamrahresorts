CLARITY & ASSURANCE

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential insights to help plan your Himalayan retreat at Amrah Resort.

Is swimming pool access free for all staying guests? ▼

Yes! Access to our hilltop swimming pool is 100% complimentary for all residing guests staying in both our Riverside Swiss Cottages and Glamping Tents. Clean poolside loungers and towels are provided.

How far is Jumpin Heights Bungee Jumping and Ganga Rafting? ▼

Jumpin Heights Bungee Jumping is located just 2.5 KM (a 5-minute drive) from our resort in Mohan Chatti. Ganga River Rafting pickup points (Shivpuri 16km & Marine Drive 26km) are directly arranged by our resort desk with vehicle transfers.

Can small cars (Swift, i10, Baleno) easily reach the resort? ▼

Absolutely! The entire road from Tapovan across the Neelkanth bridge to Mohan Chatti is a smooth, wide tar road (pucca highway). All small cars, sedans, SUVs, and tempo travelers easily reach right up to our resort gate, where ample free parking is available.

Do mobile network and Wi-Fi work in Mohan Chatti? ▼

Jio and Airtel have strong 4G/5G mobile tower connectivity across our property. We also provide high-speed Wi-Fi in the main resort dining and lobby areas.

Are pets allowed at Amrah Resort? ▼

Yes, we are a pet-friendly nature retreat! Well-behaved pets are welcome in our open camping grounds and designated Swiss cottages with prior notification at the time of booking.
